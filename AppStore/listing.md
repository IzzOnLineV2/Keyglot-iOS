### Description (max 4000)
```
Keyglot understands the message you received and helps you answer in your words, right where you already type. No copy, no paste, no switching apps.

WHERE IT WORKS
Keyglot works everywhere you type, WhatsApp, Messages, email, and more.

TRANSLATE IN PLACE
Type your message, tap a language, and it becomes a natural translation, not a literal one. The source language is auto detected, so any language to your chosen one just works. You pick which languages appear on the keyboard.

FIX AND IMPROVE YOUR WRITING
Writing in a language that is not your own? Polish your message before you send it, in the same language, no translation. Or restyle the tone: Improve, Professional, Friendly, Flirty.

VOICE NOTES YOU CAN'T FOLLOW
Share a voice note to Keyglot and read what it says, dialects like Moroccan Darija included. You get the meaning first, the exact words underneath.

LISTEN AND TRANSLATE
Hold the phone between you, press, let them speak, and read what they said in your language. Add the home screen widget for one tap access. Read the translation aloud when you need to.

TWO WAYS TO GET THE AI
- Custom, free forever: bring your own provider and API key (Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or OpenRouter). Requests go straight from your phone to the provider.
- KeyGlot Pro, a subscription: the AI is included, nothing to set up, no separate accounts.

PRIVACY
Your API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on this device and shared only with the Keyglot keyboard. In Custom mode, messages go straight to the provider you chose. In KeyGlot Pro, requests pass through the Keyglot service with no message retention, and there is no account to create.

OPEN SOURCE
Keyglot is open source (MIT). If it helps you, an optional one time tip supports the work.

KeyGlot Pro is an auto renewable subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings.
```

## App Review notes (English, into the "Notes" field)

```
Keyglot is a custom keyboard plus a share extension, a widget, and a Listen screen.

ENABLING THE KEYBOARD
1. Open the app once.
2. iOS Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard > Keyglot.
3. Tap "Keyglot" in that list and enable "Allow Full Access". Full Access is REQUIRED because
   the keyboard needs network access to reach the AI. Keys are stored only in the iOS Keychain.
4. In any chat, tap the globe to switch to Keyglot, then tap a language flag to translate, or a
   tone (Improve / Professional / Friendly / Flirty) to rewrite in the same language.

NO ACCOUNT
There is no sign up and no login anywhere in the app.

HOW TO TEST THE AI (two modes)
- Custom mode (free, bring your own key): in the app go to About/Home > Advanced > Custom, choose a
  provider and paste an API key. For your convenience a test key is provided in the "App Review
  Information > Notes" attachment / below. [ACTION: paste a temporary API key here, or a Gemini key
  for the voice features, so the reviewer can test without their own account.]
- KeyGlot Pro (subscription): use a Sandbox tester to subscribe, then translation and voice work
  with no key.

VOICE FEATURES
"Listen & translate" and shared voice notes use Google Gemini for audio. In Custom mode add a
Gemini API key (Advanced > Voice notes key). In KeyGlot Pro they work with no key.

SUBSCRIPTION
KeyGlot Pro (monthly and yearly, auto renewable) unlocks the included AI. A separate one time
"Support" purchase is an optional tip and unlocks nothing.

OPEN SOURCE
The client is open source (MIT). Entitlement is verified server side from the StoreKit signed
transaction; the app also uses App Attest to prove it is a genuine build.
```

> IMPORTANT before submitting: paste a working test API key (or a sandbox path) in the review notes,

## Privacy nutrition labels (App Store Connect > App Privacy)

Recommended answers:

- **Do you collect data from this app?** The developer (IzzOnLine) does not collect or store user
  data. Choose "Data Not Collected" IF you keep the backend zero retention (current design).
- Clarify in the review notes and privacy policy that: in Custom mode, message text and audio are
  sent directly to the third party AI provider the user configured (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or
  OpenRouter) solely to produce the translation; in KeyGlot Pro, they pass through the Keyglot
  service and are not retained. Neither is linked to an identity (there is no account).
- No tracking, no ads, no analytics SDKs.

If Apple's form pushes you to declare the content sent for functionality, use the "Data Not Linked to
You > (not used for tracking)" path and describe it as processed to provide the translation, not
stored. Keep the wording identical to the website privacy policy.
