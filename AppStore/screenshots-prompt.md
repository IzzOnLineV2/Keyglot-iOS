# Prompt per Claude Design, screenshot App Store di Keyglot

Incolla il blocco qui sotto in Claude Design. Genera 6 "poster" verticali per l'App Store.
Dopo, esporta a 1320 × 2868 px (iPhone 6.9", slot obbligatorio). Per il 6.5" puoi riesportare a
1242 × 2688 px senza rifare il layout.

---

```
Design a set of 6 App Store screenshot posters for an iPhone app called "Keyglot", a translation
keyboard. Portrait, 1320 x 2868 px each (iPhone 6.9"). They must look like one cohesive campaign.

BRAND KIT (use exactly)
- Fonts: Instrument Serif for headlines (emotional, large), Instrument Sans for everything else.
- Colors: warm canvas #FAF8F6, ink #17151C, secondary ink #5A5462, accent violet #6B4EF0.
  Dark surface for some posters: gradient #1B1725 to #241C36 with text #F7F4FA.
- Brand gradient (cyan to violet to pink): #22C3D6 -> #7A5BF0 (52%) -> #F0609B. Use it ONLY as an
  accent: the app mark, an active language chip, a 2pt "seam" underline beneath a translation, or a
  single call to action. NEVER as a full page background and NEVER behind body text.
- App mark: a rounded blue tile showing two keycaps, Arabic ain and Latin A. Keep it small.
- No em-dashes anywhere in the copy. Use commas or periods.

LAYOUT (same skeleton on every poster)
- Top third: a short serif HEADLINE (1 to 2 lines) plus a one line Instrument Sans SUBTITLE.
- Below: a single iPhone 17 Pro Max device frame (titanium/black, portrait) containing a clean
  recreation of the relevant app screen, described per poster. Screens use the app's warm/dark UI.
- Generous side margins, calm composition, lots of breathing room. Alternate backgrounds for
  rhythm: posters 1, 3, 5 on the warm canvas; posters 2, 4, 6 on the dark plum surface.
- Keep the headline area clear of the device frame; do not crop text.

THE 6 POSTERS (Italian copy first, English in [brackets] as a second locale to produce after)

1) HEADLINE: "Capisci ogni messaggio. Rispondi con parole tue."
   SUB: "Traduzioni naturali, proprio dove scrivi."
   SCREEN: a chat where an incoming message in Moroccan Darija (Arabic script, right aligned) is
   answered by a natural reply in the user's language, with a thin gradient "seam" and a small
   "Keyglot" label between them.
   [EN HEADLINE: "Understand every message. Reply in your words."] [EN SUB: "Natural translations,
   right where you type."]

2) HEADLINE: "Traduci in ogni app, non solo in chat."
   SUB: "WhatsApp, Messaggi, email e altro."
   SCREEN: the Keyglot keyboard bar at the bottom, a row of language chips (English, Francais,
   Darija, العربية) with one chip active on the brand gradient, above a message being replaced in place.
   [EN HEADLINE: "Translate in every app, not just chats."] [EN SUB: "WhatsApp, Messages, email and more."]

3) HEADLINE: "Note vocali che non capisci?"
   SUB: "Condividile e leggi cosa dicono, dialetti inclusi."
   SCREEN: a result card, an eyebrow "Traduzione" in accent violet, a large readable translation,
   the gradient seam, then "Parola per parola" with the original Darija text (right aligned) below,
   dimmer. Two small buttons: "Leggi ad alta voce" and "Copia".
   [EN HEADLINE: "A voice note you can't follow?"] [EN SUB: "Share it and read what it says, dialects included."]

4) HEADLINE: "Parla faccia a faccia."
   SUB: "Premi, ascolta e leggi nella tua lingua."
   SCREEN: the Listen and translate screen, a large circular mic button, two small pills above it
   labelled "loro parlano" (Auto) and "tu leggi" (Italiano), on the dark surface.
   [EN HEADLINE: "Talk face to face."] [EN SUB: "Press, listen, and read it in your language."]

5) HEADLINE: "Scrivi in un'altra lingua?"
   SUB: "Correggi e rifinisci prima di inviare."
   SCREEN: the same message shown before and after a "Migliora" rewrite, with four small tone
   chips: Migliora, Professionale, Amichevole, Flirty.
   [EN HEADLINE: "Writing in another language?"] [EN SUB: "Fix and polish before you send."]

6) HEADLINE: "AI inclusa con KeyGlot Pro."
   SUB: "Oppure usa la tua chiave, gratis."
   SCREEN: the KeyGlot Pro screen, the gradient app mark, three checkmarks (keyboard translation,
   voice notes, listen and translate), two plan rows (Annuale highlighted with a small "Risparmi"
   badge, Mensile), and a gradient "Abbonati" button. Do not print specific prices.
   [EN HEADLINE: "AI included with KeyGlot Pro."] [EN SUB: "Or bring your own key, for free."]

DELIVERABLE
- 6 posters at 1320 x 2868, consistent style, ready to export as PNG.
- Produce the Italian set first, then an English set reusing the same layouts with the [EN] copy.
- Avoid the word "unlimited". Keep all copy free of em-dashes.
```

---

## Note pratiche

- **Dimensioni:** l'App Store ora richiede solo il **6.9"** (1320 × 2868). Se vuoi anche il 6.5"
  (1242 × 2688) riesporti gli stessi poster ridimensionati.
- **Numero:** puoi caricarne da 1 a 10 per lingua. 6 e' un buon set; i primi 2 sono i piu' visti.
- **Screenshot reali vs ricreati:** Claude Design ricrea le schermate come mockup (coerenti col
  brand). Se preferisci gli screenshot VERI dell'app dentro i frame, fai gli screenshot col
  simulatore (iPhone 17 Pro Max, dark) e sostituisci le schermate ricreate nei poster.
- **Localizzazione:** il prompt genera IT + EN. Per le altre lingue del listing (es, fr, de, pt-BR,
  ja, ko, zh-Hans, ar) puoi chiedere a Claude Design di duplicare i poster traducendo solo headline
  e sottotitolo (te li preparo io se servono).
- **Coerenza store:** le headline riprendono la copy dell'app e del listing (niente "unlimited",
  niente trattini lunghi, "funziona ovunque, non solo WhatsApp").
